Takeaways

What the coast tells us

Australian tourism is coastal tourism. Most visitors are Australians making short trips to a nearby coast. They travel in seasons that run in opposite directions in the tropics and the south. Together they support almost nine in ten tourism jobs, so a bad season on the coast is a bad season for the whole industry.
